---

## Data Collection

NightscoutTV does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, usage data, diagnostic data, or analytics with the developer or the developer community.

All configuration data you enter — including your Nightscout URL and access token — is stored exclusively on your own Roku device using the Roku registry.  
This data never leaves your device except as part of direct API requests made by the app to your own Nightscout server instance, which you own and control.

---

## Data at Download and Installation

NightscoutTV acknowledges that downloading the software from GitHub or installing it from the Roku Channel Store may involve data collection by those respective platforms, governed entirely by their own privacy policies.  
Such incidental data exposure is beyond the scope of this policy and outside the developer's control.

---

## Your Nightscout Data

NightscoutTV displays glucose and treatment data retrieved directly from your personal Nightscout server.  
This data is fetched in real time and displayed on screen. It is not cached beyond the current session, not transmitted to any third party, and not accessible to the developer in any form.
